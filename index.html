Create Tournament Escrow

Deposit a prize into contract escrow and register the competitive bracket parameters.

ℹ
Rules & Constraints: Prize must be greater than zero and is parsed using BigInt arithmetic without loss of precision. Roster and Bracket URLs must be HTTPS from allowlisted domains on two different hosts. Registration closes the day before bracket_date.

Authorize Player Handle

Organizer only: bind an esports player handle to an authorized wallet address.

ℹ
Organizer Only: Only the tournament creator can authorize handles. Handles must be 3 to 24 characters (letters, numbers, dot, underscore, dash). Player address cannot be zero or the organizer address. Registration closes the day before bracket_date.

Register Player Entry

Authorized player only: claim your authorized competitive handle from your wallet.

ℹ
Authorized Player Only: You must connect the exact wallet address that the organizer authorized for this handle. Prize will only be paid out to registered handles. Registration closes the day before bracket_date.

Settle Tournament

Trigger GenLayer AI web consensus to verify the winner across both public sources.

ℹ
Public Settlement: Anyone can call settle on or after the declared bracket_date. GenLayer validators scrape and read both the roster URL and bracket URL. The prize is paid only if both pages name the same registered handle. If the winner is unregistered or sources disagree, the tournament remains open for organizer reclamation after the claim date.

Return Unclaimed Prize

Return unclaimed escrowed prize funds back to the organizer after the grace period.

ℹ
Reclamation Rule: If a tournament remains OPEN past the claim_after date (the UTC day after bracket_date), anyone can trigger return_unclaimed to release the escrowed GEN prize back to the organizer's wallet.
